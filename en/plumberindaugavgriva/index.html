Plumber in Daugavgrīva, Riga

A plumber in Daugavgrīva works mainly in post-war apartment buildings put up in a former closed military zone. The district is low-lying, a large part of it is in the 1% flood risk zone, and an RTU study names it among the places where the water is more often cloudy. Both conditions shape what residents run into most often.

From wooden housing to post-war apartment buildings

After the Second World War Daugavgrīva became a closed military zone, most of the wooden housing was demolished, and apartment buildings were built in its place. So many of the buildings are of a similar age, and their pipes are either old or have been replaced piece by piece.

The risers in Daugavgrīva buildings belong to all the owners together, and their replacement, as well as how to pay for it, is decided by the owners' meeting. An apartment owner can sort out their own part: the branch pipes up to the riser, taps, toilet and drains. If the owners replace the risers, it is good value to replace the apartment branches at the same time, because the boxing and walls are already open.

Replacing the branch pipes in an apartment from the risers to the bathroom and kitchen costs from €500 for labour, excluding materials; pipe repair of a single damaged section is from €35. The price depends on whether the pipes run exposed or inside the wall and how many connection points the apartment has.

The owners' right to decide on risers: LV portāls, 24.10.2024, referring to Sections 3 and 16 of the Apartment Property Law (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums). Checked 05.11.2026.

The far edge of the network: why the water tends to be cloudy

About half of Riga's water comes from the Baltezers groundwater reservoir, and the other half is treated from Daugava surface water. Measurements by RTU scientists in 2021 show that the risk of cloudy water is higher in more distant districts, including Daugavgrīva, Bolderāja and Vecmīlgrāvis.

In an apartment the sediment particles are caught by mixer tap aerators, the screen at the inlet, the washing machine inlet screen and the water heater (boiler) tank. So after a period of cloudy water, problems show up not throughout the system but in these places.

  • A tap starts spluttering or runs weakly: clean the aerator; if that does not help, mixer tap repair from €25.
  • Weak pressure throughout the apartment: low water pressure diagnosis from €25.
  • The water heater heats more slowly or hisses: water heater cleaning from €35.

If cloudy water runs throughout the building, the cause is in the city or building network, and the house manager must be told.

Risk of cloudy water in more distant districts: RTU 2021, LV portāls. Water sources (Baltezers and the Daugava): LSM, 2018. Checked 05.11.2026.

The 1% flood zone and shallow groundwater

The districts portal states that a very large part of Daugavgrīva is exposed to a 1% flood risk. In the lowest places groundwater is 0–1.5 m deep, and the ground contains silt and peat.

In an apartment building this mostly affects the basement, where the shared sewer and the water inlet run. High groundwater can seep in through damaged pipes and foundation joints. If water regularly stands in the basement, the building benefits from a sump pit with a sump pump; its repair costs from €75.

When the surrounding water level is high, the sewer in the basement can start to back up. First make sure the outlet is not blocked. If it is clear but the water still comes back, check whether the basement outlet needs rebuilding.

The treatment plant in the district and a smell in the apartment

Riga's waste water treatment plant, BAS “Daugavgrīva”, is in Daugavgrīva between the Buļļupe and Ziemeļupe rivers. There Rīgas ūdens also accepts waste water brought by septic haulers from septic tanks and holding tanks.

If a sewage smell is noticeable only in the apartment and not in the yard, the cause is usually in the building's plumbing: a dried-out trap, a damaged toilet seal or a blocked vent stack. Smell diagnosis costs from €35.

When the building replaces its risers: what to prepare in the apartment

If the owners in a Daugavgrīva building have decided to replace the risers, it is worth the apartment owner preparing a few things before the day of the work, so that the job does not drag on and nothing more is opened than needed.

  • Clear access to the riser boxing or recess and find out whether the tiles around it will have to come off.
  • Decide whether you will also replace your own branch pipes and valves beyond the riser at the same time; doing it later will be harder.
  • Check whether the towel rail is connected to the hot water riser; if so, its connection will be affected, and towel rail replacement costs from €80.
