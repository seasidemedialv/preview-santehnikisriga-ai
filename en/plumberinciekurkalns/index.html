Plumber in Čiekurkalns, Riga

A plumber in Čiekurkalns is often dealing with one question right now: how to prepare a house for the new city sewerage that Rīgas ūdens is building on Čiekurkalna 1. līnija and its cross streets. The district also has wooden workers' houses and post-war prefab blocks each with their own kind of work. Here we go through it all in turn.

New sewerage on 1. līnija and the cross streets: scope and timing

Rīgas ūdens plans to extend sewerage on Čiekurkalna 1. līnija and the cross streets. The first stage includes 2,260 m of gravity sewer and 2,255 m of water main renewal, with construction in 2026–2027, for about 130 properties. The second stage will start from the end of 2027 and cover more than 50 properties, almost 200 households in total.

As early as 2023 a new water main was built on Saulkrastu iela and Stienes iela, and sewerage on Saulkrastu iela, Liepupes iela and Stienes iela with three pumping stations, so houses on these streets can already connect.

In the streets Rīgas ūdens will lay the networks here using trenchless methods to protect the trees. The connection pipe on your plot is built separately, and its route is worth choosing so that it goes around trees, paths and fence foundations.

Network scope, stages and the 2023 construction: Rīgas ūdens, “New water and sewer networks planned in Čiekurkalns”. Checked 05.11.2026.

The road to a connection: technical conditions and co-financing

Everything starts with technical conditions from Rīgas ūdens, provided for in paragraph 19 of Cabinet Regulation No. 174. They can be requested by the developer, any of the co-owners or the manager of an apartment building, and Rīgas ūdens prepares them free of charge within 11 working days. Then come the design and building the connection pipe from the street network to the house.

In Riga the municipality co-finances connecting a residential house to city sewerage in all districts except the phase 4 and 5 project districts, which have their own rules (Binding Regulation No. RD-23-207-sn, in force since July 2023). The application must be submitted within 3 years of completing the connection, so keep the invoices and work certificates from the start.

Technical conditions: Cabinet Regulation No. 174, the Rīgas ūdens page on properties without a connection. Co-financing: Riga Binding Regulation No. RD-23-207-sn (as explained by Rīgas ūdens). Checked 05.11.2026. Before applying, check the current conditions on the Rīgas ūdens website.

Inside the house before the switch-over

A pipe in the street does not mean the house sewerage is ready for it. In an old wooden house the drains often go to several places: the kitchen to one pit, the bath and toilet to another. Before the switch-over they have to be collected into one outlet that leaves the building at the position and height set in the design.

  • We check that the internal pipes have the right fall and are not cracked.
  • We disconnect the old pipe to the septic tank or holding tank so that waste water does not go in two directions.
  • If the water main in the same street is also being renewed, we look at the house inlet and its shut-off valve.

The cost of the internal work can only be given after seeing the house, and we agree the price before we start. Drain cleaning before the switch-over starts from €40.

Separating the combined system in the Teika and Čiekurkalns area

In phase 3 Rīgas ūdens separated the combined sewer systems in the Teika and Čiekurkalns area: it built the “Sirius” storm water collector and foul sewerage on Ropažu iela. In a combined system rainwater and household waste water flow through one pipe; in a separated system each has its own.

If you do not know where the roof gutters and yard rainwater from your house go, it is worth finding out before the connection design, because the design has to show the two flows separately.

Peat by the Šmerļupīte and the dry residential area

All four types of geotechnical area are found in Čiekurkalns. By Rusova iela, the railway and the Šmerļupīte stream, groundwater is closer to the surface than 1.5 m, and there are peat deposits 1–2 m thick. In the residential area itself groundwater is deeper than 3 m.

For most houses this is an advantage: water does not get in the way in the connection trench, and basements are drier. By the Šmerļupīte it is the other way round: in peat the pipe needs a stable bed, and the trench may have to be pumped out.

Next to TEC-1 and post-war prefab blocks

Riga's TEC-1 power plant, built in 1954–1958, is in Čiekurkalns, and post-war prefab blocks have survived next to the wooden houses. In an apartment building with central heating every radiator is connected to the shared system, so the house manager's consent is needed before replacing one; how to arrange this is explained in the article on radiator replacement approval.

In a prefab block the everyday jobs are different from those on the līnijas streets: leaks under the bath, a blocked kitchen branch, a dripping mixer tap and an old cistern.
